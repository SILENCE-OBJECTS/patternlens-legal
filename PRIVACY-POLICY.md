## 1. What PatternLens is

PatternLens is a privacy-first observation app. It helps you capture situations as “Objects” and view structural descriptions of rhythms and patterns. It does **not** diagnose, treat, coach, or provide medical or therapeutic advice.

## 2. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| Account | Email, user ID (e.g. Sign in with Apple) | Create and secure your account |
| App content | Objects, notes, ritual / session metadata you enter | Provide the core observation features |
| Purchases | StoreKit transaction identifiers, subscription status | Process in-app purchases / restore |
| Diagnostics | Crash logs, performance (if enabled) | Keep the app reliable |
| Device / technical | App version, OS version, coarse device type | Compatibility and security |

We do **not** collect HealthKit data. We do **not** sell personal data. We do **not** use your data for cross-app tracking (no ATT tracking for ads).
